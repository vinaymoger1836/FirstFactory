**Primary sources read directly:**
- **KERC Combined Tariff Order 2025** (27 Mar 2025), full 567-page text. Source: the copy hosted by OpenCity; the original is from KERC. It gives:
  - HT-2(a) demand and energy charges and LT-5 rates for FY26/27/28;
  - HT-2 time-of-day adjustments;
  - compulsory ToD at ≥ 500 kVA;
  - LT supply below 150 kW / 201 HP;
  - minimum HT contract demand of 25 kVA.
- **IS 1172:1993**: water 45 / 30 l per head per day for factories, and the factory water-closet scale.
- **Karnataka Industrial Policy 2025-30**: rainwater harvesting mandatory for building structures.
- **Scholl SALBP data set (1993)**: G5 instances and optima.

**What I could not confirm, and why it is marked verify:**
- **Tariff (most important):** the KERC review order of 3 Mar 2026 raised HT-2(a) to ₹365/kVA and ₹6.70/kWh, and LT-5 to ₹5.20/kWh plus ₹165/HP. Only news reports were available (ThePrint/PTI, ETV Bharat). ThePrint notes the order did not state an effective date. I made these the active FY2026-27 values, verify, and kept the order's own FY26-27 values (350 / 6.60) as `superseded` history.
- **BESCOM true-up:** +₹0.56/kWh for consumption from 1 May 2026 to 30 Apr 2027. Its applicability to HT-2(a) is not stated.
- **Electricity tax:** unknown.
- **Labour:** the gazette PDFs of the OSH Code and the Central Rules 2026 (G.S.R. 345(E), 8 May 2026) were not reachable. Commentaries give 8 h/day, 48 h/week, overtime cap 144 h/quarter and overtime at 2× wages. The Central Rules apply to the central sphere. **Karnataka's own rules under the OSH Code were not found.**
  - The Factories (Karnataka Amendment) Act 2023 had 12 h/day and 145 h per three months.
  - Spread-over is disputed (one source says 12 h, another says none). I kept 12 h at confidence 0.4.
- **Welfare:** the canteen threshold conflicts (100 in most commentaries, 50 in one). The first-aid ratio was not found.
- **Wages:** the 22 May 2026 revision (₹20,350–32,145/month by zone and skill, basic + VDA ₹1,030.80) comes from an HR summary. Other summaries show lower, basic-only figures.
